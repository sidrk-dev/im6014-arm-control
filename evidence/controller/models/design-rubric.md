# Architecture rubric

Score each 0-5 with concrete findings, choose strongest base, propose grafts.

1. Canonical joint units and external/internal conversion ownership.
2. Acquisition before real open, alias and crossprocess exclusion, resource/error lifetime and preserved injection points.
3. Explicit coherent transitions, perjoint validation/age/error and no automatic recovery/rearm.
4. Truly bounded immutable target storage and sequencewatermark, invalid commands leave acceptedstate unchanged.
5. Small coherent APIs, sameexistingbenchsnapshot/startup aliases and bytes, no dual physicalownerpaths.
6. Milestone staging and acceptanceproof; no premature physicalmotiondispatch, profileframework, IPC or unmeasuredratereports.
