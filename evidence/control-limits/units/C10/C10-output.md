#### Design Decisions & Contradictions
- **Dual Velocity Streams**: `position_step_velocity` is computed from actual inward emitted endpoints using exact rationals and finite float boundaries, while `encoded_velocity` is the canonical wire velocity feedforward. Neither stream is equated to the other.
- **Quantization Stall vs. Deliberate Hold**: If $h > 0$ and $q_{\text{goal}} == q_{\text{prev}}, \dot{q}_{\text{goal}} == 0$, the controller successfully produces an unchanged deliberate hold. If $v_{\text{desired}} \ne 0$ but cadence $h$ is too small to advance by even one discrete raw step, the controller executes a conservative Stop (`DecisionCode.CONSTRAINT_VIOLATION`), preventing indefinite virtual motion drift.
- **Authority Commit**: Preparing a plan never mutates confirmed references; dispatch failure on joint 1 commits joint 0's reference while preserving joint 1's prior reference.
